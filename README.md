# ♾️ Infinito | Email Outreach & ICP Dashboards Suite

An enterprise-grade, multi-workspace web application designed for **Automated Spreadsheet Data Import, Data Cleaning, Document Schema Mapping, ICP Qualification, Email Analytics, and Executive Dashboards**.

Allows teams to manage four independent, isolated data dashboards supporting persistent browser storage, repeated spreadsheet uploads with automated deduplication, **CSV & XLSX Exporting**, and **Share via Email**.

## 🌟 Infinito Dashboards

### 1. ⚡ **Auto Studio (`index.html`)**
- Supported formats: `.csv`, `.xls`, `.xlsx`, `.json`, `.tsv`.
- 5-Step Interactive Pipeline: `Upload` → `Preview & Sheet Select` → `Clean & Validate` → `Auto Dashboard` → `Export`.

### 2. 📧 **Overall Emails Sent (`overall_emails.html`)**
- Complete cumulative email-sending dataset across all imports.
- Document-driven schema: Contact Name, Email, Company, Designation, Phone, Website, Location, Email Status, Qualification, Create Date.
- Search, multi-column filters, pagination, CSV & XLSX export, and executive KPI cards.

### 3. 🎯 **ICP 1 — Indian IT (`icp1.html`)**
- Target Profile: Indian IT Services & Product Companies (Tier 1 & Tier 2 Cities).
- Qualification Engine: Categorizes Tier 1 / Tier 2 city hubs, verifies tech decision makers.

### 4. 🚀 **ICP 2 — Indian Enterprise (`icp2.html`)**
- Target Profile: Indian Enterprise Buyers (manufacturing, BFSI, retail, logistics, healthcare).
- Excludes pure IT Services companies (flagged for review).

### 5. 🌐 **ICP 3 — Global SME Business (`icp3.html`)**
- Target Profile: Small & Medium Businesses **worldwide across any country** (US, UK, Canada, Germany, Australia, UAE, Europe, Asia, Global).

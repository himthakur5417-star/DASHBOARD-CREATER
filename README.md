# ♾️ Infinito | Multi-Workspace & Compliant Lead Generation Suite

An enterprise-grade, multi-workspace web application designed for **Automated Data Upload, Data Cleaning, Quality Diagnostics, Dynamic Workspaces, and Compliant Lead Generation & Verification**.

Allows teams to manage five independent, isolated data workspaces plus a dedicated Lead Generation Studio, each supporting persistent database storage, repeated spreadsheet uploads with automated deduplication, **Share via Email**, and **Standalone HTML Exporting**.

## 🌟 Infinito Workspaces & Lead Generation

### 1. ⚡ **Auto Studio (`index.html`)**
- Supported formats: `.csv`, `.xls`, `.xlsx`.
- 4-Step Interactive Pipeline: `Upload` → `Preview & Sheet Select` → `Clean & Validate` → `Auto Dashboard`.

### 2. 📧 **Overall Emails Sent (`overall_emails.html`)**
- Starts in a clean zero-data initial state.
- Persistent cumulative database storage: Merges new valid records while skipping duplicates.
- Import Summary log, Chart.js 4 circular email metric donut charts, and Contact Intelligence table.

### 3. 🎯 **ICP 1 — Indian IT (`icp1.html`)**
- Target Profile: Indian IT Services & Product Companies (Tier 1 & Tier 2 Cities).
- Qualification Engine: Categorizes Tier 1 / Tier 2 city hubs, verifies tech team size, decision makers.

### 4. 🚀 **ICP 2 — Indian Enterprise (`icp2.html`)**
- Target Profile: Indian Enterprises buying AI & Business Applications (Revenue ≥ ₹100 Crore or 250+ employees).
- Excludes pure IT Services companies (flagged for review instead of deleted).

### 5. 🌐 **ICP 3 — Global SME Business (`icp3.html`)**
- Target Profile: Small & Medium Businesses **worldwide across any country** (US, UK, Canada, Germany, Australia, UAE, Europe, Asia, Global) with 50+ headcount.
- Flexible global location structures: Country, State/Province, City, Postal Code, Timezone.

### 6. 🔍 **Compliant Lead Generation Studio (`lead_gen.html`)**
- 5-Stage Guided Workflow: `Requirements` → `Search & Extract` → `Deduplicate` → `Verify & Score` → `Review & Save`.
- Verification & Confidence Scoring Engine: Website reachability, email status (`Verified`, `Valid format`, `Invalid`, `Not available`), phone & LinkedIn verification.
- Save approved leads directly into ICP 1, ICP 2, or ICP 3 workspace databases without auto-contaminating datasets.
